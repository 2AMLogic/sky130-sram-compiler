## Why the slice is measurable: pin interface and probe nodes

The flat extraction renames nothing for free, so the pair agrees structurally instead: the layout
top carries one top-cell label per bench-visible net, and the four probed storage nodes
(`Q00`/`QB00` of cell (0,0), `Q01`/`Q02` of the row-0 half-selected cells (0,1)/(0,2)) are labelled
where the bitcell's own li1 pin label sits, then declared in `--pins` — so the extracted `.SUBCKT`
exports them as cleanly renamed pins and the benches measure `v(q00)`-style top-level nets on both
legs. Same treatment keeps `WL0`, `WLO`, `WLIN0`, `SBL`, `SBLB`, `DOUT`, `PCHB`, `SAE`, `WEN`, `CS0..3`,
`BL0..3`, `BLB0..3`, `DIN`, `VDD`, `VSS` addressable; the header order the pinned klt writes is the
order the generated schematic header declares (`pin_order_mismatch` was null). In-cell labels below
the top (every bitcell's `Q`/`QB`/`WL`/…) stay internal named nets, demoted by `--pins`.

Timing in the ladder's own resolution: the read-access transition sits inside the first 50 ps step on
both legs (no measurable SAE-delay shift at this corner); the write completion transition moves one
to two steps — bounding pulse width 0.35–0.40 ns pre-layout vs 0.45–0.50 ns extracted, i.e. write
margin at tt/1.8 V/25 °C grows by ≤ 0.15 ns (>0.05 ns, ≤0.15 ns) on this slice. No transition falls
outside the tested range on either leg (read ladders have both ends, write ladders have a failing
shortest pulse on both legs). Half-select retention holds on both legs; the stored-0 level of the
watched half-selected cells rises 0.103 → 0.123 V and the read Q/QB bump grows ~17 % (0.106 → 0.124 V)
with parasitics — the sense differential at the long delay shrinks ~5 % (|1.478| → |1.409| V,
|1.296| → |1.231| V).

The physical R/C behind the deltas is the extraction's own lumped model (one series R per net terminal
+ one ground C per net, vertical-overlap coupling; no `--critical-net` lateral coupling was declared):
the bit lines, sense lines and the row-0 word line now carry their drawn-wire R and C, where the
pre-layout bench had none (the README's "Not modelled (pre-layout)" row). Representation effects are
not separated out (no `--device-only-leg`): the wldrv finger split and per-finger AS/AD fold into the
delta alongside the parasitics.

## Controls

- negative control (WEN never asserted): never flips on either leg — the extracted leg's worst
  stored-1 sample is 1.7993 V vs 1.7999 V schematic (sub-mV droop over the 20-trial run).
- LVS negative control (above) mismatches; the DRC deck reports clean.
- each side actually exercised the target cell: every leg's `q` ladder samples `v(q00)` (the stored
  node of cell (0,0)) with values at both rails, and the read benches' `bump`/`dv` samples move as
  the parasitics land — a silently-disconnected DUT cannot produce those (the `flat_dut_mismatch` /
  `pin_count_mismatch` pre-flight checks were null, so the instantiation line was valid on both legs).

## Scope

Provisional, single-corner (tt, 1.8 V, 25 °C), single instance of the bench slice, lumped-RC extraction,
no Monte Carlo — the post-layout corner grids and mismatch evidence are future work (as the pre-layout
grids are the committed `klt sim` requests). Not analog item 7: that manifest citation stays absent
until the post-layout evidence tier is met. Not the SNM benches (#210) and not the full macro (whose
extract-only capacity record is `20261121-3c2d73b.md`). No tool gap found: the pinned klt 0.9.0
represented the matched slice and every probe, so the issue's gap route (upstream filing) was not
needed; the one friction worth noting — flat extraction sanitises label dots and joins multi-label
nets with `|`, which is why the probe nodes are declared pins rather than dotted internal names — is
documented behaviour (`docs/cli/extract.md`), not a defect.

## Addendum 2026-11-24: word-line wire R/C truncated at the drawn span

Disclosing a scope limit the sections above leave implicit: the extraction's word-line R/C covers
only the drawn 4-column span (`SLICE_COLS = 4` in `design/compiler/gen_layout.py`). The 124
omitted columns contribute gate load (`XWLLOAD`, m=248, in the bench body outside the extracted
top) but no wire parasitics, so the series R and C of the full macro's longer word line are
modelled by neither leg. The reported write-completion shift (≤ 0.15 ns) is therefore expected to
be a lower bound on the full-macro figure: more word-line R/C should slow WL rise and stretch the
required write pulse. That direction is inferred, not measured — the post-layout tier that could
measure it is #207's future work.
