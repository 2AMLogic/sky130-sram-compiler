## Which engine sees the marker

Same three decks, same conclusions as the 2026-10-17 record: the curated
deck is marker-blind; `sky130A.lydrc` references 81/2 in a few well/implant
rules only; `sky130A_mr.drc` switches li, licon, mcon, poly, diff/tap,
nsdm/psdm, via and others on 81/2 and is the only deck implementing
core/periphery rule switching for the cells that matter here. The
`coverage_unknown` caveat carries over unchanged: a "0" below is "no
violation reported", never "proven clean" — the coverage block of
klayout-engine rows lists rule categories but no checked layers, for zero
and non-zero rows alike. Evidence that the mr deck really executed is the
stripped-marker control (51).

## `m1.4` vs `791_m1.4`: which rule fired, and did the finding move?

(Corrected form of the old issue's step 3: these are two distinct rules in
the byte-identical mr deck — line 1007-1008 dimensional `791_m1.4 : min. m1
enclosure of mcon : 0.03um`, line 1011-1012 topological `m1.4 : mcon
periphery must be enclosed by m1` — so the question is which one fired and
whether the finding moved, not whether one was renamed.)

Measured today on the stripped-marker foundry case:

- mr deck: 16 fires, **all `791_m1.4`** (the dimensional check, external);
  the topological `m1.4` fires **0**. 16 distinct bboxes, all thin mcon/m1
  enclosure-edge shapes on the foundry cell (dbu, ×0.001 µm):
  (390,1860,395,2090), (565,1860,570,2090), (750,1860,755,2090),
  (925,1860,930,2090), (1110,1070,1115,1300), (1285,1070,1290,1300),
  (1470,280,1475,425), (1470,365,1475,510), (1645,280,1650,425),
  (1645,365,1650,510), (1830,280,1835,425), (1830,365,1835,510),
  (2005,280,2010,425), (2005,365,2010,510), (2640,564,2655,786),
  (2640,1584,2655,1806).
- `.lydrc`: its `m1.4` — the only rule of that name in that deck, with the
  identical description `min. m1 enclosure of mcon : 0.03um` — fired the
  **same 16 bboxes, 16/16 identical** to the mr deck's `791_m1.4` bboxes.

So both decks' rows are the **same dimensional check under two deck-local
names**. The old record lists `m1.4 12` in *both* decks with the same count
(12 = 12 then; 16 = 16 now; geometry identical where visible). The
evidence-backed reading: the violation class — m1 under-enclosure around
mcon — is the **same finding** as 2026-10-17. What moved is (a) the fired
count 12 → 16 (+4 instances) and (b) the mr deck's *reported name* for it,
now the deck-declared dimensional `791_m1.4`. **Not determined on this
host**: whether the old mr row's `m1.4 12` already denoted the dimensional
check under the old engine's naming, or the topological rule fired 12 then
and 0 now. The old report DB was not kept, and neither klt `0.7.1.dev1755`
nor standalone klayout `0.28.16` is restorable here (host-wide tools; no
installs). Count parity across decks then, bbox identity now, and zero
topological fires today support the first reading; the second cannot be
excluded. Either way the marker finding itself is unaffected: the count
moved in the stripped and edge rows only, i.e. where strict rules apply.

## Verdicts (stated plainly)

Unchanged in substance from the 2026-10-17 record, with current counts:

- **Curated sky130 deck: wrong for marker-bearing cells, by omission.** It
  ignores the marker; the foundry bitcell fails with 102 violations even
  with its marker. One more than 2026-10-17 — the newly enforced
  `poly.space.1` fires once on the foundry cell, a curated-deck revision,
  not marker behaviour.
- **`sky130A.lydrc`: unspecified for this purpose.** It fails the foundry
  cell (60) with its marker. Do not use it to sign off marker-bearing
  geometry.
- **`sky130A_mr.drc`: correct inside and outside, per-shape at the edge,
  with one seam hole.** 0 inside / 51 outside / 37 at the edge. Inside the
  marker the strict logic rules are not enforced (probe: 3 violations
  hidden); a shape crossing the edge is checked both ways (li.8 slivers);
  spacing between a core-side and a periphery-side shape across the edge is
  checked by neither li.3 nor li.7 (probe, edge in the gap: 0.15 um clean).
  Observed for li only.
- The silent-wrong-rule-set direction stands: a logic-rule cell placed under
  81/2 is checked to the relaxed rules and passes with violations the logic
  rules would reject.

## Answers for the spec

Same three answers as the 2026-10-17 record (read there in full), with 51/37
in place of 47/33: keep the 81/2 boundary in li-free space and construct
≥ 0.17 µm li spacing across it, because neither deck checks cross-edge li
spacing; review the seam by a purpose-built check, not a green DRC; and only
`sky130A_mr.drc` with the `--deck-var feol=true beol=true thr=1` trio, plus
a stripped-marker negative control per run, signs off a marker-bearing array
— the curated deck cannot.
