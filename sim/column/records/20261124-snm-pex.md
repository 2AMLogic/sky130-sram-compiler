## Physical explanation of each DC delta

Capacitance cannot explain a DC shift (`I = C dV/dt = 0` at steady state: `CBL0 = 14.65 fF`, `CBLB0 = 14.58 fF`, `CIN = 0.436 fF`, `COUT = 0.139 fF`, `CPCHB = 1.267 fF`, `CQ = 0.466 fF` per cell). As a direct experimental check, re-running the identical `klt pex` flow with `--parasitic-variant 'drop-r:*'` (retaining every extracted ground and coupling capacitor while shorting all star resistors) reproduced `device-only` to 16 decimal digits (`hold_eye_snm = 0.707997 V`, `read_eye_snm = 0.429394 V`), proving **100%** of the `extracted - device_only` shift is resistive.

1. **Device-representation delta (`device-only - schematic`: `+0.003 mV` hold, `+0.014 mV` read)**:
   `sram_snm_halfcell_col64.spice` carries schematic `nrd`/`nrs` (`0.6905` on `XMPL`/`XMAL`, `0.3452` on `XMNL`) and nominal `ad`/`as`/`pd`/`ps`, whereas `klt extract` omits `nrd`/`nrs` and emits layout-measured `AD/AS/PD/PS` (e.g. shared drain `AD=0.1491 um^2` on `X$1` vs `0.2436 um^2`). Without intrinsic `nrd`/`nrs`, `device-only` closely matches the original committed `col_{hold,read}_snm.sp` benches (which also omit `nrd`/`nrs` on the half-cell: `0.707997 V` vs `0.707997 V` hold; `0.429394 V` vs `0.429397 V` read).
2. **Read SNM resistive delta (`extracted - schematic = -0.459 mV` / `-0.107%`; `extracted - device_only = -0.473 mV` / `-0.110%`)**:
   With `WL0 = 1.8 V` and `PCHB = 0 V`, a steady read current flows from `VDD` through precharge `X$382` (plus equaliser `X$384`/`X$383`), bit line `BL0`, access `X$2`, node `OUT`, and pull-down `X$1` to `VSS`. At `IN = 1.8 V`, `I_read = 143.655 uA`:
   - `ROUT_t2 = 18.470 Ω` adds `+2.653 mV` from `X$1`'s drain (`out__t2 = 144.492 mV`) up to the `OUT` hub (`147.145 mV`).
   - `RVSS_t0 = 13.775 Ω` adds `+1.979 mV` of source degeneration at `X$1`'s source (`vss__t0 = 1.979 mV`), slightly reducing `V_GS` and raising `V_SB` on `X$1`.
   - Series resistance on the access/bit-line/precharge path (`ROUT_t1 = 32.489 Ω` -> `+4.667 mV`, `RBL0_t0 = 33.061 Ω` -> `-4.750 mV`, `RBL0_t1 = 32.284 Ω`, `RVDD_t2 = 26.092 Ω`) reduces `I_read` slightly, partially offsetting the pull-down rise for a net `+2.735 mV` (`+2.673 mV` vs schematic) increase in `V(out)` at `IN = 1.8 V`.
   - Because `V(out)` is probed at the `OUT` hub (the node that feeds the opposite inverter's gates in a closed cell), this lifted low-state floor plus slight transition softening shrinks the read butterfly's inscribed square by `0.459 mV` (and `read_pinned_snm` by `2.239 mV`).
3. **Hold SNM resistive delta (`extracted - schematic = -0.136 mV` / `-0.019%`; `extracted - device_only = -0.138 mV` / `-0.020%`)**:
   With `WL0 = 0 V`, `X$2` is off so DC current is zero at the rails (`V(out) = 1.800000 V` and `0.000000 V` identically). Static current flows only through the `X$255`/`X$1` inverter during its switching transition (`IN ~ 0.5-1.0 V`), where source degeneration (`RVDD_t0 = 26.267 Ω` on `X$255`, `RVSS_t0 = 13.775 Ω` on `X$1`) and drain star legs (`ROUT_t0 = 43.660 Ω`, `ROUT_t2 = 18.470 Ω`) slightly reduce small-signal inverter gain (`ext - sch` flips from `-0.189 mV` at `IN = 0.720 V` to `+0.332 mV` at `IN = 0.774 V`), narrowing the hold eye by `0.136 mV`.

## Extraction model (verbatim from `pex-sram_snm_halfcell_col64.report.json.gz`)

```json
{
  "capacitance": "net-to-ground for every net's own (non-coupled) area/perimeter, plus net-to-net for the vertical-overlap coupling `coupling` describes below -- a coupled net pair gets a direct capacitor between their two hub nodes, not just capacitors to the deck's ground/substrate net",
  "coupling": "vertical overlap (crossover) unconditionally -- where one net's conductor on an adjacent metal level sits directly over another *distinct* net's conductor, that overlap area is charged between the two nets instead of to ground (issue #760) -- plus lateral (same-layer, sidewall) coupling, but only for a net pair naming one of the caller's declared `--critical-net` nets (issue #976): facing-edge length within that layer's own minimum-spacing lookback is charged between the two nets, *additively* (not deducted from either net's substrate fringe term, unlike the vertical case -- a known simplification). Any same-layer pair with neither side named `--critical-net`, and fringe shielding in general, are still not modelled",
  "resistance": "single lumped series resistance per net, distributed as a star across that net's device terminals (issue #592) -- not a per-segment, distributed RC ladder, *unless* `--distributed-rc` names this net via `--critical-net` (issue #977, Epic #709 Phase 2b): then the net's terminals are ordered along their approximate physical spread and its total R/C is broken into a chain of per-segment resistors (segment length proportional to inter-terminal distance) with a ground capacitor at each terminal node (proportional to its adjacent segment length/2), instead of one star hub -- still an approximation (terminal position is a device-placement proxy, not true per-segment routing geometry), but a strictly finer-grained one than the single-hub star",
  "frequency": "quasi-static -- one frequency-independent R and C per net; no skin effect, no distributed transmission-line behavior"
}
```
